## Purpose
This policy sets the conditions under which Meridian Health Partners employees may work remotely instead of at a designated office.

## Eligibility
Remote work is available to employees in non-clinical roles who have completed 90 days of employment and have manager approval. Clinical and front-desk roles are not eligible.

## Work Hours
Remote work is limited to a maximum of 2 days per week. Employees must be reachable during the core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone.

## Equipment
Remote work is bring-your-own-device. Meridian does not provide a stipend or reimbursement for home office equipment under this policy.

## Approval Process
Employees submit a remote work request to their manager, who approves or denies it within 5 business days. Approval must be renewed every 6 months.
