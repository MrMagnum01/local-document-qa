## General
Solstice Systems sells two products: the SecureLink VPN client and the CloudVault backup tool. Both are sold as a single combined subscription; they cannot be purchased separately.

## Pricing
The combined subscription is billed per seat, monthly or annually. Annual billing gets two months free compared to paying monthly for twelve months. There is no free tier; a 14-day trial is available on signup.

## Account Management
Seats are added or removed from the admin console under Billing > Seats. Removing a seat immediately revokes that user's SecureLink and CloudVault access but keeps their CloudVault backup snapshots for 30 days before deletion.

## Technical Support
Support is ticket-based only, through the customer portal, for all products. There is no live chat and no phone line.

## Cancellation
Subscriptions can be canceled anytime from Billing > Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; there are no partial refunds for the unused portion of a period.
