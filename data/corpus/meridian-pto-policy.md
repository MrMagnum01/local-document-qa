## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.25 days per month, or 15 days per year. Part-time employees accrue a prorated amount based on scheduled hours. PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment but cannot be used until after 30 days.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1 unless local law requires otherwise.

## Requesting Time Off
PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned absences. Requests for fewer than 2 days' notice require manager approval and are granted only if staffing allows.

## Unused PTO Payout
Upon separation from the company, employees are paid out for unused accrued PTO up to a maximum of 10 days, at their final base rate of pay.
