## Reimbursable Expenses
Reimbursable business expenses include travel (airfare, mileage at the IRS standard rate, lodging), client meals up to $50 per person, and approved training or certification fees.

## Submission Process
Expenses are submitted through the finance portal with itemized receipts attached within 30 days of the expense. Expenses submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed except by CFO exception.

## Approval Thresholds
Expenses under $200 are auto-approved after manager sign-off. Expenses of $200 to $1,000 require director approval. Expenses over $1,000 require CFO approval regardless of category.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, and home office equipment are not reimbursable under this policy. Home office equipment for approved remote employees is instead covered under the separate Remote Work Policy's home office stipend, where applicable.
