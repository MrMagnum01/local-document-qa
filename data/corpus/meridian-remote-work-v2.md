## Purpose
This policy sets the conditions under which Meridian Health Partners employees may work remotely instead of at a designated office. It replaces the version 1.0 remote work policy effective 2025-06-01.

## Eligibility
Remote work is available to employees in non-clinical roles who have completed 90 days of employment and have manager approval. Clinical and front-desk roles are not eligible. Fully remote arrangements now require additionally a director-level sign-off.

## Work Hours
Employees in approved fully-remote roles may work remotely 5 days per week. Employees without director sign-off remain limited to 2 days per week. All remote employees must be reachable during the core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone.

## Equipment
Meridian provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for approved remote employees, covering desk, chair, or monitor purchases. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of approval.

## Home Office Stipend
The $500 stipend introduced under this policy is separate from, and not deducted against, the general expense reimbursement limits in the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Approval Process
Employees submit a remote work request to their manager, who approves or denies it within 5 business days. Fully-remote requests also require director sign-off within 10 business days. Approval must be renewed every 6 months.
