## Setup
Each terminal is provisioned centrally and assigned to a store location before shipment. On first power-on, staff enter the store's activation code from the manager portal to bind the terminal.

## Daily Reconciliation
At close of business, staff run "End of Day" from the terminal menu, which totals cash and card transactions and compares them against the expected drawer amount. Discrepancies over $10 must be logged in the manager portal.

## Processing Refunds
Refunds require a manager PIN for any amount over $100. Refunds to a card must use the original card; cash refunds are permitted for cash purchases under $100 without manager approval.

## Error Codes
Error E01 indicates a card reader connection failure; restart the terminal. Error E12 indicates the terminal has lost sync with the manager portal and transactions will queue locally until connectivity is restored. Error E30 indicates a receipt printer paper jam.

## Hardware Maintenance
Clean the card reader slot weekly with a dry contact cleaning card. Receipt paper should be replaced only with the thermal paper specified in the hardware kit; other paper can damage the print head.

## Support Contact
Store hardware issues go through the internal IT helpdesk, not the public customer support FAQ.
