## Return Window
Items may be returned within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt or order confirmation.

## Condition Requirements
Returned items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached. Items showing signs of use are not eligible for return.

## Refund Method
Refunds are issued only to the original payment method. Cash refunds for card purchases are not available.

## Exceptions
Final-sale and clearance items cannot be returned. Gift cards are non-refundable.
