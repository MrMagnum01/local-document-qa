## Return Window
Items may be returned within 45 days of purchase with a valid receipt or order confirmation. This extends the 30-day window from the prior version of this policy. Electronics are an exception: they must be returned within 14 days.

## Condition Requirements
Returned items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags attached. Items showing signs of use are not eligible for return.

## Refund Method
Refunds may be issued to the original payment method, or as store credit if the customer prefers store credit and the item is not a final-sale item. Cash refunds for card purchases are still not available.

## Exceptions
Final-sale and clearance items cannot be returned. Gift cards are non-refundable. Electronics follow the 14-day window stated above rather than the general 45-day window.
