## Benefits Enrollment
New employees can enroll in health, dental, and vision benefits within 30 days of their start date. Outside of that window, changes are allowed only during the annual open enrollment period or after a qualifying life event.

## Parental Leave
Meridian offers 12 weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver, following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## Remote Work FAQ
Questions about eligibility, approved remote days, and the home office stipend are governed by the current Remote Work Policy, not by this FAQ. Employees should consult that policy directly, since remote work terms have changed over time.

## Payroll Schedule
Employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last business day of the month. Direct deposit is required; there is no paper check option.
