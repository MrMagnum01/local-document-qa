## Order Issues
For missing or damaged items, contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number. Cascade will send a replacement or issue a refund per the current Returns and Refunds Policy.

## Warranty
Electronics sold by Cascade carry the manufacturer's warranty only; Cascade does not offer an extended warranty program. Warranty claims go directly to the manufacturer.

## Store Locations
Store hours and addresses are listed on the Cascade website store locator. Holiday hours may differ from standard hours and are posted at each store.

## Loyalty Program
The Cascade Rewards program gives 1 point per dollar spent. 100 points equal a $5 reward. Points expire 12 months after the last purchase on the account.
